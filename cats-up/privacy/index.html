Privacy Policy — Cats Up

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Cats Up ("the app", "we", "us") is a clipboard manager and file drawer published by JeJiEgypt. This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores everything only on your device. This policy applies to Cats Up for macOS and Windows.

No data collected, no data shared

Cats Up does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. Your clipboard contents, screenshots, recordings, and files never leave your device. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs or trackers, and it requires no account, sign-in, email, or login. It does not access or use any device identifier or advertising ID. The app makes no network requests to send your information anywhere; the only time it touches the network is when you click a link inside a captured item, which opens that link in your default web browser.

What the app does on your device

To work as a clipboard manager and file drawer, the app accesses the following — all locally, and only to display items back to you:

  • Clipboard: it watches your system clipboard so that text, links, and images you copy appear in the drawer.
  • Screenshots & screen recordings: it watches the folder you choose for screen captures so new screenshots and recordings appear in the drawer.
  • Downloads: it watches your Downloads folder so newly downloaded files appear in the drawer, ready to drag into other apps.
  • Folders you choose (Local tab): it shows the contents of folders you explicitly add, so you can browse and drag files out of them.

On macOS, access to your screenshot folder and any folders you add is granted only through folders you select yourself, using the system's security-scoped bookmarks. The app never accesses files you have not granted it.

What the app stores on your device

Everything the app keeps is saved locally, in the app's private on-device storage, and never leaves your device. This includes:

  • The clipboard items you capture — copied text and links, copied/screenshot images, and screen recordings;
  • Metadata about downloads that appear in the drawer (such as file name and path);
  • The folders you add to the Local tab and their display labels;
  • Your settings — the chosen screenshot folder, window size, panel options, and your chosen app language.

This data is stored only for the app to function on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Your data stays on your device

Because the app stores personal clipboard and file information, that data is intentionally kept on-device only. It is not uploaded to any server controlled by us, and the app has no cloud sync. You remain in control: you can delete any item, clear a whole category, or remove a folder at any time. The app's managed copies (clipboard images and screen recordings) are deleted when you delete the corresponding item. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the stored information is permanently deleted. Your own files on disk (downloads and the files inside folders you add) are never deleted by clearing the drawer — only the app's own captured copies are.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
