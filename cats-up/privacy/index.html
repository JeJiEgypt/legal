Privacy Policy — Cats Up

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This policy covers the macOS version of Cats Up, distributed through the Mac App Store. The Windows version has its own policy: Cats Up Desktop for Windows — Privacy Policy.

Cats Up ("the app", "we", "us") is a clipboard manager and file drawer published by JeJiEgypt. It runs entirely on your own Mac: no user accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything it captures stays on your device, with one exception you control: a bug report you choose to send (see What leaves your device).

No tracking, no accounts, no third-party analytics

Cats Up does not collect, sell, or share personal or usage data, and it does not transmit your clipboard contents, screenshots, recordings, or files anywhere. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs or trackers, and it requires no account, sign-in, or login. It does not access or use any device identifier or advertising ID. The only data that ever leaves your Mac is a bug report you deliberately fill in and send — described under What leaves your device below. Aside from that, the network is touched only when you click a link inside a captured item, which opens that link in your default web browser.

What the app does on your device

To work as a clipboard manager and file drawer, the app accesses the following — all locally, and only to display items back to you:

  • Clipboard: it watches your system clipboard so that text, links, and images you copy appear in the drawer.
  • Screenshots & screen recordings: it watches the folder you choose for screen captures so new screenshots and recordings appear in the drawer.
  • Downloads: it watches your Downloads folder so newly downloaded files appear in the drawer, ready to drag into other apps.
  • Folders you choose (Local tab): it shows the contents of folders you explicitly add, so you can browse and drag files out of them.

On macOS, access to your screenshot folder and any folders you add is granted only through folders you select yourself, using the system's security-scoped bookmarks. The app never accesses files you have not granted it, and it does not scan or index the rest of your disk.

By default, a screenshot the app captures is removed from your screenshot folder once its copy has been added to the drawer, so the capture lives in one place instead of two. If you would rather keep both, turn on Keep the original file under Images in Settings: the app still collects the screenshot, and the file macOS saved stays in your screenshot folder too.

You can switch off any capture type — text, images, videos, links, or files — in Settings. With images off, for example, your screenshots stay in your screenshot folder untouched, exactly as if the app were not running.

What the app stores on your device

Everything the app keeps is saved locally, in the app's private on-device storage, and never leaves your device. This includes:

  • The clipboard items you capture — copied text and links, copied/screenshot images, and screen recordings;
  • Metadata about copied and downloaded files that appear in the drawer (such as file name and path) — only a reference; the file itself is never copied or moved;
  • The folders you add to the Local tab and their display labels;
  • Your settings — the chosen screenshot folder, window size, panel options, sort order, which capture types are enabled, and your chosen app language.

This data is stored only for the app to function on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Please understand what a clipboard history means. If you copy a password, a recovery code, or anything else sensitive, it is saved as plain text like any other copied text. Delete such items from the app when you are done, or turn off text capture in Settings before copying them.

Your data stays on your device

Because the app stores personal clipboard and file information, that data is intentionally kept on-device only. It is not uploaded to any server controlled by us, and the app has no cloud sync. You remain in control: you can delete any item, clear a whole category, or remove a folder at any time. When you delete an item, the app's own managed copies (clipboard images and screen recordings) are sent to the Trash where possible, rather than destroyed. Your own files on disk — downloads, files you copied, and the files inside folders you add — are never deleted by clearing the drawer; only the app's own captured copies are. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the stored information is permanently deleted.

What leaves your device

One thing, and only if you choose to send it: a bug report.

If you open Report a bug and press Send, the app transmits what you typed — the subject, the description, and your email address if you chose to provide one — together with the app version and your platform name (darwin). It is sent over HTTPS to Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com), a third-party form-to-email relay, which forwards it to the developer's inbox at jejiegypt@gmail.com. It is used only to answer and fix the problem you reported. See Web3Forms' privacy policy for how they handle that message in transit.

Nothing else is sent. The app makes no other network requests: no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs or trackers, and no update checks of its own (updates are delivered by the Mac App Store). No clipboard content, screenshot, recording, file, or folder information is ever transmitted — not to us, not to anyone. The only other time the network is involved is when you click a link in a captured item, which opens it in your default browser.

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared. A bug report you chose to send stays in the developer's email inbox; write to jejiegypt@gmail.com and it will be shown or deleted on request.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed to children and collects nothing about who is using it.
